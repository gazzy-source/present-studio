# Quality Bar

The gates every artifact clears before it is emitted. Runnable, not aspirational.

An engine runs these against its own output. An artifact that fails any **hard
gate** is not emitted — it is revised or the gap is reported. Soft gates are
weighted judgment.

---

## Hard gates

Failure means the artifact does not ship. No exceptions, no averaging.

### G1 — Traceability

Every factual claim about a product traces to Collection DNA or Brand Pack.

**Test:** list every claim. Point each to its source. Any claim without a source
is deleted or the artifact is blocked pending founder input.

**Never** invent: materials, fibre content, origin, factory, process, heritage,
dates, collaborators, pricing, availability.

### G2 — Anti-signals

No anti-signal from `premium.md` is present.

**Test:** scan for urgency, superlatives, self-described luxury, exclamation
marks, discount-as-identity, generic sensory adjectives, stated emotion.

### G3 — Substitution

Replace the brand name with a plausible competitor. The artifact must break.

**Test:** if it survives substitution unchanged, it is category-generic. Reject.

### G4 — Contract conformance

The artifact satisfies every required field and validation rule of its contract.

**Test:** run the contract's conformance checklist. All required fields present
and non-placeholder.

### G5 — Voice conformance

The artifact obeys the Brand Voice's registers, cadence, vocabulary and
prohibitions.

**Test:** run the voice's own conformance checklist. Any explicit prohibition
present is an automatic fail.

---

## Soft gates

Weighted. An artifact should clear at least **four of six**. Three or fewer means
revise before emitting.

### S1 — Specificity density

At least one verifiable concrete detail per paragraph, or per beat in
time-based work. Adjective-heavy passages fail.

### S2 — Cuttability

Removing 30% loses nothing essential. If every line is load-bearing, the artifact
is dense enough. If not, it was padded.

### S3 — Sensory grounding

At least one non-visual sense engaged — weight, texture, temperature, sound,
air. Visual-only work reads as a lookbook caption.

### S4 — Tension

One nameable tension, statable in a sentence. Frictionless work is forgettable.

### S5 — Restraint

One idea per surface. Multiple competing ideas mean it should have been multiple
artifacts.

### S6 — Silence

The artifact leaves something unsaid that the audience completes. Fully closed
work has nothing for the audience to do.

---

## Scoring

```
HARD GATES    G1 G2 G3 G4 G5      all must pass
SOFT GATES    S1 S2 S3 S4 S5 S6   ≥ 4 of 6
```

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| All hard pass, ≥5 soft | Emit |
| All hard pass, 4 soft | Emit, note the weak gate |
| All hard pass, ≤3 soft | Revise once, then emit or report |
| Any hard fail | Do not emit. Revise, or report the blocker |

An engine that cannot clear a hard gate reports the gap. It does not lower the
bar to pass, and it does not fabricate to satisfy G1.

---

## The revision rule

Revision means **subtraction first**. Cut, then sharpen nouns, then restructure.
Adding material to fix a failing artifact almost always worsens it — most
failures are failures of excess.

Two revision passes maximum. An artifact failing after two passes has a problem
upstream: a weak brief, a missing DNA input, or the wrong archetype. Report that
rather than continuing to polish.

---

## What this file is not

Not a style guide and not a checklist to be performed ceremonially. If an
artifact passes every gate and is still lifeless, the gates were satisfied
mechanically. Gates catch failure; they do not manufacture quality.

Judgment remains required. The bar is the floor.
