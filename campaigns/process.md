# Archetype: process

```yaml
id: process
version: 1.0.0
status: stable
beats: 5
intent: show how it is made, as the argument for what it costs
```

## When to use

Craft is the story. A material, a technique, a factory, a hand. Strongest where
the process is genuinely unusual and Collection DNA can verify every step.

The most claim-dense archetype and therefore the most legally exposed. Gate G1 is
absolute here.

---

## Shape

| # | Function | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `establish` | The raw state. Material before it is anything |
| 2 | `escalate` | Hands. The work happening. Specific, not montage |
| 3 | `rest` | The pause in the making. Waiting, drying, resting, setting |
| 4 | `turn` | The transformation completes. Raw becomes garment |
| 5 | `close` | The finished piece, in the world it was made for |

**Turn at 4 of 5.**

---

## Required inputs

- `collection-dna` — `construction`, `materials`, `provenance`, all `verified`
- `visual-identity` — `light` and `lens`; macro capability required
- `brand-voice` — product register

---

## Rules

1. **Every step must be `verified`.** `provisional` facts may not appear in a
   process campaign at all — this archetype reads as documentary, and audiences
   treat it as factual claim. Report the gap rather than softening.
2. **No invented craft.** Never imply hand-work that is machine-work, or local
   production that is not. This is a factual and legal failure, not a stylistic
   one (principle 8).
3. **Hands must be real and directed.** Generic hands-on-fabric is the failure
   state. Specify the actual action.
4. **Beat 3 is what makes it premium.** Anyone can film making; showing waiting
   signals a process that cannot be rushed.
5. **No copy on beats 1–3.** Let the work speak. Copy on 4 or 5 only.

---

## Common failure

Process becomes generic craft-montage — sewing machine, scissors, steam, folded
garment — which any brand could run and which fails the substitution test
outright (G3).

The test: does any beat show something specific to *this* maker? If every frame
could be any factory, the archetype is being used as decoration.
