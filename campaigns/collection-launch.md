# Archetype: collection-launch

```yaml
id: collection-launch
version: 1.0.0
status: stable
beats: 7
intent: introduce a collection as a world, not a range
```

## When to use

A new collection or drop enters the world. The audience does not yet know the
thesis, the season, or why these pieces exist together.

**Not** for a single piece (`single-piece`), a restock, or a brand-level
statement (`manifesto`).

---

## Shape

| # | Function | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `establish` | The world. Widest frame. Product absent or incidental |
| 2 | `establish` | The hour and the weather. Still holding product back |
| 3 | `escalate` | First piece enters, as consequence of the world |
| 4 | `rest` | Silence. No subject. The collection's texture — material, surface |
| 5 | `escalate` | Second and third pieces. Range becomes visible |
| 6 | `turn` | The thesis lands. Why these exist together |
| 7 | `close` | Consequence. Not a restatement of beat 1 |

**Turn at 6 of 7.** Late turn is correct here — the audience must accumulate the
world before the thesis means anything.

---

## Required inputs

- `collection-dna` — thesis and tension are load-bearing
- `brand-voice` — narrative register
- `visual-identity` — full
- 3–5 pieces at status `production` or `available`

---

## Rules

1. **Product does not appear before beat 3.** Leading with product converts the
   campaign into a catalogue (principle 4).
2. **The thesis is never stated as copy.** Beat 6 lands it through image and
   juxtaposition. Explaining it is the failure mode.
3. **Beat 4 has no subject.** The texture beat is where fabric weight and surface
   do the selling, and it is the beat most often cut in review. Defend it.
4. **Maximum three copy beats.** Seven beats of copy is a script, not a campaign.
5. **Range is shown, not listed.** Beat 5 implies breadth; it does not enumerate.

---

## Common failure

The collection thesis gets stated in beat 1 as a caption, and every subsequent
beat illustrates it. This inverts the archetype — the audience is told the
conclusion, then shown evidence for a claim they were never allowed to form.

If beat 1 contains the thesis, the campaign has already failed.

---

## Renders to

`short-form-reel` (compressed to 5–6 beats), `campaign-film`, `still-series`.
