# Archetype: manifesto

```yaml
id: manifesto
version: 1.0.0
status: stable
beats: 6
intent: state what the brand believes, without stating it
```

## When to use

Brand-level, not collection-level. Launch, repositioning, season opening, or a
moment where the brand needs to be understood rather than shopped.

Used rarely. A brand that manifestos every quarter believes nothing — the
archetype's force comes from scarcity.

---

## Shape

| # | Function | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `establish` | The world as it is. Often without product entirely |
| 2 | `escalate` | The friction. What the brand finds intolerable |
| 3 | `rest` | Silence. The longest beat. Let the friction sit |
| 4 | `escalate` | The alternative, shown not argued |
| 5 | `turn` | The stance becomes undeniable |
| 6 | `close` | The world, changed. Rhymes with beat 1 without repeating it |

**Turn at 5 of 6.** Beat 1 and beat 6 are the same place, differently seen — the
only archetype where `close` deliberately returns to `establish`'s location.

---

## Required inputs

- `brand-voice` — `stance` is the entire foundation of this archetype
- `visual-identity` — full
- `collection-dna` — optional; product may be absent entirely
- `campaign-memory` — check hard. A repeated manifesto is worse than none

---

## Rules

1. **The stance is never said aloud.** If the brand's stance appears as a copy
   line, the manifesto has become an advertisement for itself
   (`premium.md` — self-description).
2. **Product is optional and usually better absent.** This archetype sells the
   brand's position, and product presence pulls it toward commerce.
3. **Beat 3 is the longest beat.** Discomfort needs duration. Cutting it short is
   the most common way this archetype fails.
4. **Must alienate someone describable.** A manifesto everyone agrees with has no
   stance (`premium.md` — point of view).
5. **Copy on three beats maximum**, and beat 5 is stronger silent.

---

## Common failure

The manifesto becomes aspirational wallpaper: beautiful frames, elevated
language, no friction. Beat 2 gets softened in review because the friction feels
negative, and without friction beat 5 has nothing to turn.

The test: name the group this alienates. If you cannot, return to beat 2.

---

## Renders to

`campaign-film` (native), `short-form-reel` (compressed to 4 beats — cut 2 and 4,
never 3).
