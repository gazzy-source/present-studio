# Present Clothing — Brand Laws

> **Status: ACTIVE. Layer 1 — permanent.**
> This document does not version. A change here is a different brand, not a new
> release. Laws are never bent, never granted exceptions, and never overridden by
> a contract, a deadline, or a campaign.

Supporting document. Not contract-governed. Parent of
`creative-principles.md`, which is parent of `visual-identity.md` and
`brand-voice.md`, which are parent of all campaign work.

---

## The One Test

> **Does this want something from the viewer?**

Applied before anything ships. If it wants a look, a click, a feeling, an
agreement, or a purchase — it fails, however good it is. If it wants nothing, it
works.

Every law below is this test made checkable.

---

## The Laws

### L1 — Want nothing

The work asks nothing of the person encountering it. No hook, no question, no
imperative, no urgency, no bid for agreement.

**Test:** name what this asks the viewer to do, feel, or believe. If you can name
it, cut it.

**Precedence:** where laws conflict, L1 wins.

### L2 — The middle is the subject

Not the decision. Not the arrival. The interval.

**Test:** name the moment depicted. If it is a beginning or an ending, it fails.

### L3 — Nothing is conferred by outcome

No result, reveal, completion, before-and-after, or moment of triumph. Identity
is constituted by repetition, and the work shows repetition.

**Test:** name the outcome shown. If you can name one, it fails.

### L4 — Never explain

The brand does not articulate its own meaning. Not in copy, not in captions, not
in voiceover, not in a manifesto film.

**Test:** does the output state a feeling, a value, or the brand's reasoning?
Fail.

### L5 — Return, don't replace

The same piece, the same place, the same person — again. Newness is not a reason.

**Test:** name what this references that the brand has shown before. Nothing
referenced, fails.

## Derivation chain

```
Brand Laws → Creative Principles → Visual Identity / Brand Voice → Campaigns
```

Every principle cites the law it serves. Every execution rule cites the
principle it serves. Every campaign artifact cites the principle it serves.

**An orphan rule is deleted, not grandfathered.**

---

## Exceptions

**A Law is never bent.** There is no logged-exception path at this layer.

Principle-level and execution-level exceptions are permitted under the rules
stated in `creative-principles.md`, and all are logged to `campaign-memory.md`.
An unlogged exception is a failure, not a judgment call.
