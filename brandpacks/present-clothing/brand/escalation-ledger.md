---

## Purpose

Layer 3 versions freely so the brand's cinematic and written language can evolve
across decades without identity moving. This ledger is what makes that evolution
legible rather than accidental — a viewer in 2040 should be able to date a reel
to its era, and a reader of this file should be able to see exactly what changed
and why.

Without the ledger, Layer 3 drifts from Layer 1 one reasonable exception at a
time, and in five years nobody can point to where it went wrong.

---

## Rules

1. **Cadence.** Roughly every two years. Not scheduled, not mandatory — an era
   change is made when there is a reason, and no reason is manufactured to hit a
   date.
2. **One rule.** Exactly one execution rule per era, in `visual-identity.md` or
   `brand-voice.md`.
3. **Never a constant.** C1–C8 in `campaign-system.md` are outside scope.
4. **Never a law or principle.** `brand-laws.md` does not version.
   `creative-principles.md` changes only with a reason logged to
   `campaign-memory.md`, which is a different mechanism from this one.
5. **Chain intact.** The changed rule must still cite the principle it serves
   after the change. If it cannot, the change is rejected.
6. **Named acceptance.** `accepted_by` names a human.
