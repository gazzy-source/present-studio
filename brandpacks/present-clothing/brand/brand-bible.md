## The name

**Present** carries three meanings, and the brand sits where they overlap:

| Meaning | Reading |
|---|---|
| Present *(adj.)* — here, attending | The mental state |
| Presence *(n.)* — physical substance, weight, there-ness | The garment quality |
| Present *(n.)* — a gift, a thing given and kept | The object's life |

A coat with presence and a person who is present are the same word for a reason.
Weight anchors attention. That is the entire brand.

---

## Thesis

**Garments with enough physical presence to keep the wearer in the present.**

Most clothing is designed to be unnoticed — lighter, softer, frictionless. Present
Clothing designs for the opposite: weight you feel, fabric that resists, seams
with a position. Clothing that reminds you it is there, and by doing so, reminds
you that you are.

---

## Tension

**Substance disappears. Lightness demands attention.**

The contradiction the brand runs on: heavy, structured, substantial garments are
the ones you stop thinking about, while thin performance fabric asks to be
adjusted all day. Presence produces ease. Weightlessness produces fuss.

This is arguable, non-obvious, and true of well-made clothing. It is the tension
every campaign should be able to trace back to.

---

## Stance

**Clothing should return you to the present, not perform for the future.**

Rejected: aspiration, the styled life, clothing as a bid on who you might become.
Present Clothing is worn now, in ordinary weather, doing ordinary things.

**Who this alienates:** trend buyers, people who shop for the version of
themselves they intend to be, anyone who wants the garment to do the talking.
This is intentional (`foundation/premium.md` — point of view).

---

## What the brand is against

Named explicitly, because the friction is what makes the manifesto archetype
work:

- Newness as a reason to buy
- Clothing designed for photographs rather than wear
- Lightness treated as an achievement
- The word "effortless" applied to things requiring effort to make
- Ranges wide enough that nothing in them is definitive

---

## Category position

> Proposed. `unverified` — requires founder confirmation.

Small-range everyday garments and outerwear, made to archival standard. Few
pieces, repeated across seasons, revised rather than replaced.

Positioned against: mid-market elevated basics on one side, seasonal luxury
outerwear on the other. Present sits where construction is luxury-grade but the
range is deliberately small and the intent is daily wear.

---

## The world

Northern, coastal-industrial, overcast. Early morning and the hour before rain.
Concrete, weathered timber, salt air, wet stone, unheated rooms with good light.
Working buildings rather than styled interiors.

Never: sunshine, resorts, nightlife, styled apartments, motion-blurred city
energy, warmth as an aesthetic.

---

## Brand behaviours

1. **Repeat rather than replace.** The same coat, revised, for years. Newness is
   not a reason.
2. **State the weight.** Grams, ounces, dimensions. Numbers over adjectives.
3. **Never explain the feeling.** Show the weather, the hour, the hand
   (`foundation/principles.md` #3).
4. **Sell nothing at speed.** No urgency, no countdowns, no scarcity mechanics.
5. **Show the making.** Craft evidence is the price justification.
